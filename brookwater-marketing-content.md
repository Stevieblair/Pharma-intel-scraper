## Recommended Workflow

1. **Create visuals** — Use Midjourney or Canva AI for product imagery
2. **Build videos** — Use Runway ML or Pika for AI clips OR hire a creator on Billo/Fiverr for UGC
3. **Edit for platform** — Use CapCut for TikTok, Canva for Instagram/Facebook
4. **Add captions & music** — Use trending audio on TikTok/Reels, royalty-free on Facebook
5. **Schedule & post** — Use Later or Buffer to maintain consistent posting cadence
6. **Boost top performers** — Run paid ads on Instagram/Facebook behind your best-performing organic posts
